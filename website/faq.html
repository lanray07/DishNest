Good questions, clear answers

Can saved recipes work offline? Yes: the recipe book, planner, pantry and shopping list are local. Does voice always work offline? No: recognition depends on the installed device service. Can I import from any website? No: public HTTPS pages with supported Recipe JSON-LD are supported; restrictions are respected. Is OCR multilingual? The bundled model reads English; other language models are not yet included. Is this ready for submission? No: Fire hardware QA, billing verification, complete localisation and approved screenshots are still required.
