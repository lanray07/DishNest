Simple beginnings. Room to grow.

The free recipe book allows up to 25 saved recipes, with meal planning, manual entry, shopping, pantry and essential cooking tools. Import and templates can be explored in the explicitly labelled demo kitchen in this development preview. Lifetime is the main Premium offer: GBP 19.99 in the UK or USD 24.99 in the US, with annual alternatives at GBP 9.99 / USD 12.99 and monthly alternatives at GBP 1.49 / USD 1.99. Amazon catalog drafts use these approved prices; other marketplace prices are calculated by Amazon. The app will display the actual price returned by Amazon. A Cloudflare Workers Free verifier is being configured separately, but purchases remain disabled in the delivered APK until live purchase testing and release approvals pass. There is no active trial or checkout. Actual store testing, further Premium scope and publisher approval remain release blockers.
