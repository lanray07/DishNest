Updated 19 October 2026

Status: Draft; publisher legal review remains pending. Publisher: O. Bankole Project: https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest Support: https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest/issues

Draft privacy policy — the publisher is O. Bankole. Final disclosures and service-operation details still need review before release. DishNest stores recipes, meal plans, shopping items, pantry items, photos and timers in private app storage. Registration, ads and analytics transmission are absent. URL import contacts the requested website, its robots.txt policy and public HTTPS redirect destinations (including redirected policy locations). These services receive normal network request information such as your IP address and the DishNest user-agent. An allowed robots policy is not permission to copy content. Photo OCR runs locally using a bundled English model. Voice activation is push-to-talk; your selected speech provider may transmit and process audio according to its own policy. DishNest does not retain raw microphone audio. Export creates a user-selected file; JSON excludes photos and timers. ZIP includes recipe photos with a 64 MB total limit and excludes timers and purchase records. Restoring either format requires an empty kitchen. New attached photos are resized, oriented and re-encoded locally without source EXIF metadata; abandoned drafts are removed after seven days. Delete all kitchen data removes the local database contents and attached photos. No cloud sync provider is connected. Device backups are disabled in the app manifest. Exported files remain under your control after app-data deletion. Configured billing versions send Amazon user and receipt identifiers to the verification service and Amazon. The verifier processes them in memory without receipt logging; the app retains receipts and signed decisions privately for restore and bounded offline access. Deleting kitchen data preserves purchase records; uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local copy. A final service operator, retention policy and contact must be added before enabling billing.

For general support, open an issue at https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest/issues. Issues are public: do not include private recipes, photos, purchase receipts, Amazon account identifiers or other sensitive information. For private support or privacy/deletion assistance, email banksmi@mail.com. The future billing-service operator and final production service details still require publisher approval before commercial release.

Deleting local kitchen data In DishNest, open Settings and use Delete all kitchen data to remove recipes, plans, shopping items, pantry items, timers and attached photos. Exported backup files remain under your control and must be deleted separately. Kitchen-data deletion preserves purchase records; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes the local app copy. No DishNest account is required. For help, email banksmi@mail.com; do not post private content or purchase evidence in GitHub issues. DishNest cannot delete copies held by your selected speech provider or by recipients of files you explicitly share; contact those providers or recipients separately.
