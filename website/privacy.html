Your kitchen is personal

Draft privacy policy — review and add the legal publisher and support contact before release. DishNest stores recipes, meal plans, shopping items, pantry items, photos and timers in private app storage. Registration, ads and analytics transmission are absent. URL import contacts the requested website and any public HTTPS redirect destinations. Photo OCR runs locally using a bundled English model. Voice activation is push-to-talk; your selected speech provider may transmit and process audio according to its own policy. DishNest does not retain raw microphone audio. Export creates a user-selected file; JSON excludes photos and timers. Delete all kitchen data removes the local database contents and attached photos. No cloud sync provider is connected. Device backups are disabled in the app manifest. Exported files remain under your control after app-data deletion. Configured billing versions send Amazon user and receipt identifiers to the verification service and Amazon. The verifier processes them in memory without receipt logging; the app retains receipts and signed decisions privately for restore and bounded offline access. Deleting kitchen data preserves purchase records; uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local copy. A final service operator, retention policy and contact must be added before enabling billing.
