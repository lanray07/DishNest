From meal plan to shopping list

Build a grouped shopping list from the selected week. The engine scales recipes to planned servings, adds compatible ingredient amounts and deducts compatible pantry quantities. It never converts volume to weight without ingredient data. Cups remain unconverted because cup sizes differ. Add manual items, edit quantities, tick items off, and share or export as text.
