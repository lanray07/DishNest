A thoughtful weekly meal planner

Use breakfast, lunch, dinner and snack slots for every day of the week. Keep named meal plans, select the active plan, assign recipes by touch and adjust servings. Copy the previous week, save templates and generate seven dinners using saved recipe tags, total cooking time, cuisine and pantry ingredient names. Plans are deterministic and run offline; users must check dietary suitability.
