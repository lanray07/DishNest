Designed with Fire tablets in mind

DishNest uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and local Room storage, with no mandatory Google Play Services. The app requires Android API 26 or later; Fire OS versions below that are excluded. The current new-icon QA build passed six native checks, 13 UI/native smoke tests and normal/blocked-notification background timer probes on a KFTRWI Fire tablet (Android 9, API 28). A genuine Amazon-delivered private test build also launched successfully and completed a monthly test purchase and restore. Annual, lifetime and natural subscription expiry testing remain incomplete. This partial coverage does not establish compatibility with every Fire model; the full hardware and accessibility matrix remains pending. Microphone, camera and speech services are optional. Android phone layouts are included. Amazon billing is not available in this development build.
