Designed with Fire tablets in mind

DishNest uses Kotlin, Jetpack Compose and local Room storage, with no mandatory Google Play Services. The app requires Android API 26 or later; Fire OS versions below that are excluded. Twelve isolated smoke tests and normal/blocked-notification background timer probes passed on a KFTRWI Fire tablet (Android 9, API 28). This partial coverage does not establish compatibility with every Fire model; the full hardware and accessibility matrix remains pending. Microphone, camera and speech services are optional. Android phone layouts are included. Amazon billing is not available in this development build.
