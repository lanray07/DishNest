No DishNest account, login, or access code is required.

This is an incomplete development-preview draft. No publisher-signed release APK has been uploaded. Amazon purchases are disabled in current local builds; do not interpret this draft as successful IAP or production-release testing.

Core review flow for the preview:
1. Launch into an empty private kitchen. Add a recipe with a title, serving size, ingredient and cooking step. Save, reopen and edit it.
2. Assign the recipe to a day and meal slot in Meal planner. Adjust servings and generate a shopping list. Compatible quantities consolidate and compatible pantry quantities are deducted.
3. Add a pantry item and optional check date. Pantry reminders are off by default and require opt-in; device settings can delay notifications.
4. Start cooking. Check touch navigation, spoken instructions where a supported TTS provider exists, and independent timers. Denying the microphone must leave touch controls usable. Speech recognition depends on the installed provider and may use its network service.
5. Review recipe text, permitted HTTPS Recipe JSON-LD or cropped English photo OCR before saving. Check recognized text for errors. Imports and additional commercial features require Premium access. Explicit demo exploration is limited to billing-disabled development builds and is not evidence of a purchase entitlement.
6. Export a JSON or photo-inclusive ZIP backup. Restore requires an empty kitchen. Settings can delete kitchen data locally; exported files remain under user control, and kitchen-data deletion preserves purchase records.

Before release: complete live Amazon catalog/verifier testing, publisher signing and exact signed-APK device testing, privacy/legal approval, native-language review and the remaining hardware/accessibility matrix. Prepared store images are drafts pending final hardware and market approval.
