Display title
DishNest - Recipe Keeper and Meal Planner

Short description
Recipe keeper, weekly meal planner and grocery list in one place. Save recipes, organise your pantry and cook step by step with timers and spoken instructions.

Long description
Keep your favourite recipes. Plan your week. Make your grocery list.

DishNest brings your recipe book, meal planner, shopping list and pantry together in one kitchen app for your tablet. Save family favourites, organise everyday meals and follow recipes one step at a time.

YOUR PERSONAL RECIPE BOOK
Save recipes with ingredients, cooking steps, photos, serving sizes and personal notes. Mark favourites, group recipes into collections, and search or filter your recipe collection when you need dinner inspiration.

WEEKLY MEAL PLANNING
Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks across seven days. Adjust servings, copy a previous week and get offline dinner suggestions from recipes you have saved. Named plans and reusable templates help organise different routines; additional plans and templates require Premium access.

GROCERY LISTS FROM YOUR MEALS
Turn a meal plan into a grouped shopping list. Compatible ingredient amounts are combined, quantities follow planned servings and compatible pantry quantities are deducted. Add your own items, check off your groceries and share the list as text. Combining several plans into one list requires Premium access.

A MORE ORGANISED PANTRY
Track ingredient quantities and optional check dates. See recipe suggestions based on ingredient names you already have, with missing items shown. Optional daily reminders prompt you to check pantry items. Always check quantities, packaging, freshness and dietary suitability yourself.

STEP-BY-STEP COOKING
Follow large cooking steps with touch controls, spoken instructions and multiple timers. Push-to-talk commands need a compatible speech recognition service. Voice availability and internet requirements vary by device and provider. Device settings can delay timer and reminder notifications.

REVIEW BEFORE YOU SAVE
Review imported recipe text, supported recipe website data or printed English text recognized on your device. Crop a recipe photo before recognition and check the results. Website imports require internet access and respect robots policies. Import requires Premium access; save only content you have permission to use.

LOCAL STORAGE, NO ACCOUNT REQUIRED
Keep recipes, plans, pantry items and shopping lists locally without registration or advertising. Export a JSON backup or a ZIP backup with recipe photos. Restore backups into an empty kitchen; exported files remain under your control.

This development preview has Amazon purchases disabled. Premium access is required for more than 25 recipes, imports, reusable templates, additional meal plans and combined-plan shopping. Premium offers and prices are not yet available in this build.
