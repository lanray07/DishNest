DishNest privacy information - development preview
Updated 18 October 2026

Status: Draft; publisher legal review remains pending.
Project: https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest
Support: https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest/issues

Draft privacy policy — legal publisher identity and a private privacy-request channel still need review before release. DishNest stores recipes, meal plans, shopping items, pantry items, photos and timers in private app storage. Registration, ads and analytics transmission are absent. URL import contacts the requested website, its robots.txt policy and public HTTPS redirect destinations (including redirected policy locations). These services receive normal network request information such as your IP address and the DishNest user-agent. An allowed robots policy is not permission to copy content. Photo OCR runs locally using a bundled English model. Voice activation is push-to-talk; your selected speech provider may transmit and process audio according to its own policy. DishNest does not retain raw microphone audio. Export creates a user-selected file; JSON excludes photos and timers. ZIP includes recipe photos with a 64 MB total limit and excludes timers and purchase records. Restoring either format requires an empty kitchen. New attached photos are resized, oriented and re-encoded locally without source EXIF metadata; abandoned drafts are removed after seven days. Delete all kitchen data removes the local database contents and attached photos. No cloud sync provider is connected. Device backups are disabled in the app manifest. Exported files remain under your control after app-data deletion. Configured billing versions send Amazon user and receipt identifiers to the verification service and Amazon. The verifier processes them in memory without receipt logging; the app retains receipts and signed decisions privately for restore and bounded offline access. Deleting kitchen data preserves purchase records; uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local copy. A final service operator, retention policy and contact must be added before enabling billing.

For general support, open an issue at https://github.com/lanray07/DishNest/issues. Issues are public: do not include private recipes, photos, purchase receipts, Amazon account identifiers or other sensitive information. A private privacy-request channel and the legal service operator must be confirmed before commercial release.
